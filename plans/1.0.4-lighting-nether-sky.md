# 1.0.4 Lighting, nether lava, sky shape

Version **1.0.4**. One release with all four changes. Saved `sky_islands` values and codec ranges are unchanged.

## Config sliders

The customize screen shows each sky-island slider as **0–100%** across its existing min/max. 0% is the low end of that range and 100% is the high end. The stored float is still the raw value (land frequency 0.15–0.70, land size 0.40–0.80, ocean frequency 0.50–1.20, ocean size 0.60–1.20).

Labels say what the percent does: how common islands are over land, how far they spread over land, and the same for ocean. Hover tooltips state that islands follow the overworld column, that land is continentalness of at least -0.11 and ocean is below that, and that low percent is sparser or smaller. Defaults are about 36% land commonness, 38% land spread, 57% ocean commonness, and 67% ocean spread.

## Sky lighting

Stacked columns only (min y -192 and height 768). Sky islands are no longer the skylight roof for the overworld. Occlusion whose upper block is at y=321 or above is ignored when the column heightmap is built, including the floor between y=321 and y=320. Vanilla then treats the ground roof as the source, and a follow-up pass clears that false full-bright inside the sky band below each column's own island roof so island tops stay lit and interiors stay dark. Light does not propagate up from y=320 into the sky, so the overworld does not light island undersides. Downward crossing from the sky into the overworld ignores the sky block's face and applies only the destination block's opacity. Vanilla overworld and `minecraft:the_nether` are unchanged. `LightEngine.getLightDampeningInto` is not part of skylight propagation, so it is not mixed in.

## Nether lava ocean

The subterranean nether band in the stacked overworld (y=-192 through y=-65), not the separate nether dimension. For stacked noise settings, the global aquifer fluid in that band is air instead of the vanilla lava status used below y=-54. Aquifers stay on. y=-64 and above keep vanilla overworld fluids, including deep lava below y=-54. The nether-band lava-in-hole material rule is unchanged.

## Sky islands

`sky_density.json` no longer fades `end/base_3d_noise` out to a constant by about y=500, which was cutting every column off at nearly the same height. Large islands keep that 3D noise, plus a higher-frequency vertical noise, through y=530, then ease toward air by y=575 so the ceiling is not a hard slice at the build limit. Overworld ridges shift peak height. A second lerp, driven by ridges and offset noise, mixes those masses with smaller high-frequency shards. Land versus ocean still multiplies the finished sky density the same way as 1.0.1. Preset defaults are unchanged.

## Limits

- Chunks already generated keep their old terrain, aquifer lava, and saved skylight until they are regenerated or relit.
- Slider percentages are a display of the existing ranges. Worlds do not need a new `sky_islands` format.
- The nether lava change does not affect `minecraft:the_nether`.
- Sky-band air sections that never receive a skylight data layer can still read as full brightness, which is vanilla's empty-section behavior.
- New island shapes appear in newly generated sky chunks.
