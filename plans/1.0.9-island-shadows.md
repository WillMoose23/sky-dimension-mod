# 1.0.9 Island shadows

Version **1.0.9**. Sky islands no longer shade the overworld when ScalableLux is installed. Slider labels and hover text are plain language. Stored `sky_islands` floats and codec ranges are unchanged.

## What cast the shadow

ScalableLux (Starlight) computes skylight itself. It skips `ChunkSkyLightSources.fillFrom` / `update`, and its level light engine drops the vanilla sky engine after construction. The 1.0.4–1.0.8 mixins on `SkyLightEngine` and `ChunkSkyLightSources` never see the updates that draw the picture.

`SkyStarLightEngine.tryPropagateSkylight` walks each column down from the highest block and stops at the first solid island. Everything under that block, including the overworld, only receives leftover side light, so the dark patches match the islands. Sodium reads that stored skylight.

## Lighting

Stacked columns only (min y -192 and height 768).

When that walk stops at y=321 or above, a second walk starts at y=320 and treats the cell above it as open sky. Island blocks still stop light inside the sky band, so tops stay lit and interiors stay dark. The overworld is lit as if nothing exists at y >= 321.

Decreases that start in the sky stop at y=321, so an island update does not wipe overworld skylight. Flood fill does not cross between y=320 and y=321 in either direction, so overworld light does not brighten island undersides and island darkness does not sink into the overworld.

Vanilla `SkyLightEngine` / `ChunkSkyLightSources` hooks stay for worlds that are not running ScalableLux. The Starlight mixins apply only when that mod is loaded. Vanilla overworld and `minecraft:the_nether` are unchanged.

## Sliders

Labels:

- Islands above land — how many: N%
- Islands above land — how big: N%
- Islands above water — how many: N%
- Islands above water — how big: N%

Hover text says higher adds more islands, or makes them wider, over land or over water, and lower does the opposite. The percent is still the position in the existing range. Defaults are unchanged.

## Limits

- Chunks already lit keep their saved skylight until they are regenerated or relit. A new chunk is lit correctly. Changing a block at y >= 321 also repaints skylight for that one column from y=320 down.
- Distant Horizons can keep an old far-terrain image until its own cache refreshes.
- Building this mod needs the installed `ScalableLux-fabric-mc26.3-0.3.0-alpha.0.6-all.jar` on the compile classpath.
- Slider percentages are a display of the existing ranges. Worlds do not need a new `sky_islands` format.
