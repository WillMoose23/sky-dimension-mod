# 1.0.10 Sky ores

Version **1.0.10**. The exposed underside of sky islands can spawn a sparse mix of vanilla overworld ores. Lighting and slider text are unchanged.

## What changed

Ore placement is the first step of the existing sky surface pass, before the grass, sand, or mycelium cap. It only runs in the stacked column (`min y == -192`) and only from y=321 through y=575.

For each column it finds stone-like blocks with air below them, or sky-band water that this same pass then removes. Those blocks are the bottom face of an island. It replaces some of them with vanilla ore. Deepslate becomes the deepslate ore variant. Stone, tuff, granite, diorite, and andesite (`base_stone_overworld`) become the stone ore variant.

The cap still covers the top. Ore is not terrain, so the cap stops when it reaches an ore and does not turn that block into dirt or grass. A one-block island becomes ore only when that block is still stone-like. Grass, sand, dirt, sandstone, gravel, and mycelium are left alone.

## How common

Each exposed underside stone block rolls once out of 2000. Most rolls place nothing. A hit grows a small patch that stays on the underside and inside the chunk, so borders and gaps make the patches a little smaller than the sizes below.

Rough share of exposed underside stone, if the patch reaches full size:

- Coal: about 2.4%, patches of 2–4. The common ore.
- Iron: about 1.5%, patches of 2–3.
- Copper: about 0.8%, patches of 1–3.
- Gold: about 0.45%, patches of 1–2.
- Redstone: about 0.45%, patches of 1–2.
- Lapis: about 0.2%, single blocks.
- Diamond: about 0.1%, single blocks.
- Emerald: about 0.05%, single blocks.

About 6% of an exposed stone underside is ore. The rest stays stone. Diamond and emerald are much rarer than coal and iron. Emerald is not limited to mountain biomes.

A solid 16×16 underside is on the order of a handful of coal and iron blocks, with diamond about once every few chunks of underside and emerald rarer than that.

## Limits

- Already generated chunks stay as they are. Only new chunks pick this up.
- Nothing is placed below y=321 or above y=575. The overworld and the nether band are untouched.
- Undersides that are already grass, sand, dirt, or another non-stone cap do not become ore. A one-block grass or sand island stays a cap.
- Patches do not cross chunk borders.
- Worlds that are not the stacked column skip the pass.
