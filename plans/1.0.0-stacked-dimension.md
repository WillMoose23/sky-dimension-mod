# 1.0.0 Stacked Dimension

World type **Stacked Dimension**, listed with the other world types. The End and Nether portals stay the normal separate dimensions.

- Overworld column: y=-192 to y=575. Build limit y=576.
- Nether band y=-192 through y=-65, bedrock only at the bottom. No bedrock at y=-64.
- Overworld y=-64 through y=319.
- Sky islands from y=321, overworld blocks, overworld rules. Surface structures can generate on the ground and on islands.
- World spawn stays on the ground.
