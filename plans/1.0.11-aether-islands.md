# 1.0.11 Aether-like sky islands

Version **1.0.11**. Sky islands in the stacked overworld column use the Aether's skylands noise, shifted into y=321–575. Lighting, slider labels, the grass or sand cap, and underside ores are unchanged.

## Where the Aether numbers came from

Source tree `The-Aether-1.21.1-1.5.10-neoforge` (not installed in the mods folder). Terrain is not a structure or a biome mask.

- `AetherDensityFunctions` registers `aether:base_3d_noise_aether` as old blended noise: xz scale 0.25, y scale 0.25, xz factor 80, y factor 160, smear 8.
- `AetherNoiseBuilders.buildFinalDensity` adds -0.13, slides that result, adds -0.05, then blend, interpolate, and squeeze. There is no second high-frequency shard noise and no ridge mix.
- Slide call `slide(density, 0, 128, 72, 0, -0.2, 8, 40, -0.1)` and the generated `noise_settings/skylands.json` put the top fade at y=56–128 (targets 1 → 0, air offset -0.2) and the bottom fade at y=8–40 (targets 0 → 1, air offset -0.1).
- `NoiseSettings` is min y 0, height 128, size horizontal 2, size vertical 1. In 1.21 that cell size is 4 times those sizes: 8 blocks on x/z and 4 on y. The dimension type is 256 blocks tall, so the upper half of the Aether is empty sky.
- Biomes (`AetherBiomeBuilders`) only pick meadow, forest, grove, or woodland from temperature and vegetation. They do not cut the island shape. Continents, erosion, depth, and ridges are zero in the Aether router.

## What changed

`sky_density.json` is that same function, moved up by `Bands.SKY_MIN` (321):

| Aether y | Stacked sky y | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 | 321–329 | Air (bottom offset -0.1, then -0.05) |
| 8–40 | 329–361 | Underside taper, 32 blocks |
| 40–56 | 361–377 | Full density, 16 blocks. Solid where blended noise is above 0.18 |
| 56–128 | 377–449 | Top taper, 72 blocks, fading to air |
| 128–255 | 449–575 | Open sky above the layer |

Noise parameters are unchanged: xz scale 0.25, y scale 0.25, xz factor 80, y factor 160, smear 8. Interpolation cells stay 8×4. Offsets stay -0.13, then the slides, then -0.05. Beardifier is still added after squeeze so structures can push terrain. The Aether router leaves that at zero; away from structures it does not change the islands.

Horizontal scale comes from the limit-noise octave. Its lowest octave is sampled at `684.412 * 0.25 / 32768` cycles per block, about a **192-block** wavelength. The selector noise (xz factor 80, 8 octaves) wobbles coasts at about a **60-block** wavelength. Vertical smear (y multiplier × 8) keeps a column coherent, so the result is a thick mass with a rounded top and a tapering stone underside, not a stack of shards.

In the full-density band the threshold is noise > 0.18. Perlin noise in this version has standard deviation 0.270, and the limit-noise octave amplitudes put the blended value near 0.156, so about **12%** of columns are solid there. Slide math on that field: a column just over the threshold (noise 0.20) is about **25 blocks** thick, a typical solid column (noise around 0.25) is about **40–45 blocks**, and a high peak (noise 0.40) is about **70 blocks**. The mass is widest in the core and narrower at the grass top and the stone point. Gaps follow the same **~192-block** wavelength, so open sky separates the landmasses.

Default slider values leave that noise unscaled, over land and over ocean. Frequency divides by the default frequency (land 0.35, ocean 0.90) and multiplies the blended noise before the -0.13 offset, so a higher percent crosses the 0.18 threshold more often and the islands get thicker. Size divides the default size (land 0.55, ocean 1.0) into the noise xz scale, so a higher percent stretches the ~192-block pattern and the islands get wider. Codec ranges are still land frequency 0.15–0.70, land size 0.40–0.80, ocean frequency 0.50–1.20, ocean size 0.60–1.20. The 0–100% slider mapping is unchanged. Stored floats stay valid.

The 1.0.4 ridge mix and the second high-frequency blended noise are gone. Land is still continentalness of at least -0.11 and ocean is below that, but the defaults are no longer a sparser land copy of a different shape. Both defaults are the Aether layer.

The grass, sand, or mycelium cap and the underside ores still run on whatever stone this density places.

## Limits

- Already generated chunks keep their old sky terrain. Only new chunks use this density.
- One Aether-height layer sits in the lower part of the sky band. y=449 through y=575 is open sky, matching the Aether's empty upper half. Islands are not stacked up to y=575.
- Default worlds match the Aether over land and over ocean. The separate sliders still move each one away from that.
- Blocks stay vanilla stone with the existing cap, not holystone, aether grass, or quicksoil shelves. Biomes stay the stacked overworld set.
- Structures can still beard the density. The Aether's final density does not.
- The overworld, the nether band, skylight, and slider wording are unchanged.
