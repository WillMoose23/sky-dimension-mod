# 1.0.1 Sky islands

- No water in the sky band (y=321 and above). Ocean biomes can appear there and stay dry.
- Islands are sparser over land and more common over oceans, using overworld continentalness below -0.11 as ocean.
- Sky biomes are sampled separately from the ground column.
- Customize screen on the Stacked Dimension world type: land frequency, land size, ocean frequency, ocean size, and restore defaults.
- Worlds created with 1.0.0 keep the old generator. New chunks and new worlds pick up this version.
