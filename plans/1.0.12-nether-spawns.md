# 1.0.12 Nether band spawns and portals

Version **1.0.12**. The nether band under the stacked overworld (y=-192 through y=-65) is the only nether in a stacked world. New chunks get nether structures, nether features, and nether mobs there. A nether portal moves the player inside the same column.

## Root cause

Nether structures were already eligible in the stacked overworld, because the column's biome source includes nether biomes. Placement still used vanilla nether heights, and the biome test uses that height:

- Fortresses start the biome test at y=64, then slide the finished fortress into y=48–70. Both are overworld, so the nether-biome test failed. Piece building also rejects boxes at or below y=10, so the pieces still have to be assembled at the vanilla height and moved afterward.
- Bastions start at absolute y=33.
- Nether fossils sample from absolute y=32 up to `below_top`, then walk down only while above the column sea level (63). That walk never enters the band.
- Nether ruined portals pick y=27–100 and then step down until they hit solid ground, which is the overworld surface.

Nether mob lists already come from the biome at the mob's position. In this column that biome is a nether biome only below y=-64. Two overworld dimension rules still blocked the nether behavior: piglins and hoglins zombify because `piglins_zombify` defaults to true, and endermen and skeletons use the overworld darkness test.

`NetherPortalBlock` always looks up `minecraft:the_nether` and applies the 8:1 coordinate scale. The stacked preset also created that dimension.

## What spawns in the nether band

Structures, only when the column minimum y is -192:

- Fortresses, moved into about y=-144 through y=-122 (vanilla 48–70 shifted by -192).
- Bastions, started at y=-159 (vanilla absolute 33 shifted by -192).
- Nether fossils, between the shifted vanilla range (about y=-160 up to y=-67) and only while the column below the fossil is open down to the shifted nether sea (y=-160).
- Nether ruined portals, from their own structure set so they are not dropped when the shared ruined-portal set picks an overworld portal. Their in-nether heights (about y=27–100) are shifted by -192.

Mobs follow the nether biome at that position, and only below y=-64:

- Nether wastes: ghast, zombified piglin, magma cube, enderman, piglin, strider.
- Crimson forest: zombified piglin, hoglin, piglin, strider.
- Warped forest: enderman, strider.
- Soul sand valley: skeleton, ghast, enderman, strider.
- Basalt deltas: ghast, magma cube, strider.
- Fortress pieces still add blaze, wither skeleton, and the other fortress spawns.

Piglins and hoglins do not zombify in the band. Endermen and skeletons use the nether light test there (spawn while local brightness is 7 or less). Overworld and sky bands keep their own biome lists and the overworld light test.

Glowstone, debris, quartz, blackstone, nether gravel, soul sand, magma ore, fire, soul fire, and the nether open and closed springs use the nether floor and ceiling. Absolute heights that vanilla wrote for y=0–127 are shifted by -192. Fungi, deltas, and basalt columns already walk every solid layer; those attempts now sit in nether biomes when the layer is in the band.

## Portals

`stacked_dimension` no longer lists `minecraft:the_nether`. The End is still in the preset. The nether multi-noise source remains, and it is only the biome source for the band.

In a stacked column (minimum y -192 and height 768), `NetherPortalBlock.getPortalDestination` does not look up another level:

- Same X and Z as the portal. No 8:1 scale.
- At or above y=-64, including sky islands, the exit is in the nether band. Below y=-64, the exit is in the overworld band (y=-64 through y=320), never on a sky island.
- An existing portal in that band is reused (16 blocks horizontally toward the nether, 128 toward the overworld).
- Otherwise the search walks down the same column, then a 16-block spiral, for air over a sturdy floor that is not fluid. If nothing is open, an obsidian frame and portal blocks are carved at about y=-100 in the nether or on the overworld surface at that X/Z.
- The exit is the same dimension, so travel does not call `changeDimension` and does not create a nether level. Portal blocks, particles, and the portal sound stay the vanilla ones. The gamerule that blocks nether-portal travel still blocks this trip.

## Limits

- Structures and nether features appear in newly generated chunks. Already generated chunks keep their old terrain and structures.
- A world created before 1.0.12 can still have a saved nether dimension folder. Portals in a stacked column do not enter it. New stacked worlds do not create that level.
- The "entered the nether" advancement does not fire, because the player never changes dimension.
- `spring_lava` is also an overworld feature, so it stays on the overworld height range. Nether open and closed springs are the ones anchored to the band.
- Cave carvers are chosen from the biome at block y=0, so the extra carver tunnels are overworld carvers. Open space in the band comes from `nether_density`.
- Holes at y=-150 and below can still be lava, the same bottom lava sea as before. Fortresses are placed above that. Aquifers can still leave lava pockets. The 1.0.4 air fluid picker for the rest of the band is unchanged. Overworld aquifers are unchanged.
- A normal world, and `minecraft:the_nether` in a normal world, are unchanged. The End is unchanged.
- Sky-island shape, underside ores, ScalableLux lighting, and slider text are unchanged.
